What I Specialise In

Hands-on digital marketing across SEO, Ecommerce, and Paid Ads, focused on visibility, leads, and revenue.

Search Engine Optimization

I don't just optimize pages, I build a full SEO system. Starting with a deep technical audit, I map keywords to pages, fix what's broken under the hood, and build content and backlinks that compound over time. My focus is always on rankings that stick, not quick wins that fade.

Ecommerce Marketing

From setting up seller accounts on Amazon and Flipkart to running Ecommerce Ads that actually convert, I handle the full ecommerce loop. Product listings, ad creative, audience targeting, and performance tracking. Every decision is tied to one metric: revenue.

Performance Marketing & Meta Ads

I build Meta Ads campaigns from scratch; audience research, creative strategy, funnel structure, and continuous optimization. No set-and-forget. I monitor, test, and iterate until the numbers make sense.

How I Work

Process

A clear and strategic approach focused on research, execution, and continuous optimization to deliver real business results.

01
Research & Audit

I begin by understanding your business, target audience, and competitors. Through detailed research, analytics review, Google Search Console analysis, and market audit, I identify growth opportunities and performance gaps.

02
Strategy & Planning

Based on insights, I create a data-driven marketing strategy tailored to your goals. This includes channel selection (SEO, Meta Ads, Ecommerce, AEO/GEO), content planning, keyword targeting, and clear KPI definition.

03
Execution & Optimization

I implement campaigns with precision, optimizing on-page SEO, launching Meta Ads, managing ecommerce listings, and setting up tracking. Continuous monitoring ensures improved performance and better ROI over time.

04
Reporting & Growth

I track results, analyze campaign data, and provide transparent reports with actionable insights, not vanity metrics. Using performance data, I refine strategies to drive consistent growth and long-term success.
