Project Overview

The objective: Maximum WhatsApp leads at minimum cost per conversation

This was a sustained Meta Ads engagement for a real estate client — running across multiple months with 7 campaigns tested, iterated, and optimised for one single goal: generating WhatsApp messaging conversations from qualified property buyers.

The strategy combined Top-of-Funnel (TOF) WhatsApp messaging campaigns with continuous creative testing, audience refinement, and budget reallocation toward high-performing campaigns. The best-performing campaign alone generated 452 conversations — marked "High performing" by Meta — at just ₹20.52 per conversation.

🎯 Campaign Objectives
Drive direct WhatsApp conversations from interested property buyers
Keep cost per messaging conversation below ₹35
Identify the best-performing creative and audience combination
Scale budget into high-performing campaigns, pause underperformers
Build a consistent pipeline of warm leads for the client's sales team
The Strategy

Test → Learn → Scale — A systematic approach to WhatsApp lead gen

Rather than running one large campaign and hoping for results, I used a multi-campaign testing framework. Each campaign tested different audience segments, creative angles, or timing. Once winning combinations were identified, budgets were concentrated there while underperformers were paused.

Phase 01
Campaign Launch & Audience Testing

Launched initial TOF WhatsApp campaigns targeting property buyers by location, income bracket, and interest signals. Multiple campaigns ran in parallel to test which audience segments responded best to real estate messaging.

Defined target audience: age 28–55, homebuyers, investors, location-specific
Tested broad interest targeting vs. detailed behaviour-based segments
WhatsApp CTA: "Send Message" direct to client's WhatsApp Business number
Campaigns: TOF-WA-CAMP-16/08 and TOF-WA-CAMP-23/09 launched first
Phase 02
Creative Iteration & Optimisation

Based on early data, creatives and copy were iterated. Ad sets with higher CTR and lower cost per result received increased budgets. Campaigns showing zero delivery were paused early to avoid wasted spend.

Tested property showcase creatives vs. lifestyle/aspiration creatives
Copy variations: price-led, location-led, urgency-led messaging
CPC optimised to ₹3.53–₹3.91 across active campaigns
TOF-WA-CAMP-12/10 launched as fresh creative test mid-campaign
Phase 03
Scale the Winner — TOF-WA-CAMP-26/09

One campaign emerged as the clear winner: TOF-WA-CAMP-26/09. Meta flagged it as "High performing" — the highest signal Meta gives to a campaign. Budget was concentrated here. This single campaign generated 452 out of 598 total conversations — a 75.6% contribution to total results.

Key Takeaways

What this project taught me

01
Test multiple campaigns, not one big bet
Running 7 campaigns let us find the winner. Putting all budget into one campaign from day one would have missed the ₹20.52 CPL opportunity entirely.
02
Pause fast, scale fast
4 of 7 campaigns showed zero delivery — they were paused immediately. Budget saved from non-performers was redirected to the winner, compounding its results.
03
WhatsApp beats forms for real estate in India
Buyers prefer to ask questions casually before committing. WhatsApp as the conversion touchpoint consistently delivers higher-quality leads than web forms for property enquiries.
04
Meta's "High performing" badge is a signal, not a surprise
The algorithm rewards campaigns that match audience intent with relevant creative. Build for the user, not the algorithm — and the algorithm will reward you anyway.
05
CPC is an early warning system
Campaigns with high CPC (₹3.91 vs ₹3.66) showed lower efficiency downstream. Monitoring click costs early lets you reallocate before wasting meaningful budget.
06
Naming campaigns with dates matters
Campaign names with dates (e.g., 26/09, 12/10) make it easy to track what launched when, compare performance across time periods, and communicate clearly with clients.
