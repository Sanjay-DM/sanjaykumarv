How to Improve Your Google My Business Listing and Handle Reviews the Right Way

📅 2025
⏱ 6 min read
✍ Sanjay Kumar V
📍 Local SEO · GMB · Reviews

For a local business, the importance of online visibility is extremely high. Among the most effective ways of boosting your local presence is through Google My Business (GMB). If you have optimised your GMB listing, you can increase your local search rankings considerably.

In this blog, let's discuss the most important factors affecting the performance of GMB listings — Proximity, CTR, and how you should professionally respond to Google Reviews.

In this article
  • 1. Proximity — Making Google Trust Your Location
  • 2. CTR — Getting People to Actually Click
  • 3. How to Respond to Google Reviews
  • 4. What Not To Do
  • 5. Conclusion
Factor 01
1

Proximity

Proximity is one of the most crucial factors in Google's local ranking algorithm. Google needs to believe that your business actually operates in the location you've mentioned. The details you provide — address, map pin, business images, and local references — all help Google verify the authenticity of your listing.

📌 Three things to get right
🔩
Fix your service area — Service area settings on GMB can sometimes be incorrect. Fixing this helps Google understand exactly where you operate, improving local visibility.
📍
Pin accuracy — The pin on the map should be placed exactly at your business location. An off-pin confuses Google's algorithm.
🏢
Suite & local markers — If you're in a building, include the suite number. Mention nearby landmarks to help Google and users locate you precisely.
Factor 02
2

CTR — Click Through Rate

CTR plays a significant role in the success of your GMB listing. It's a direct signal Google uses to judge how relevant and useful your listing is to searchers.

If people see your listing but don't click, Google interprets it as low relevance and your ranking drops. When people do click, Google sees it as a trust signal and pushes your listing higher.

⚡ What to optimise for maximum CTR
🏷
Business title — Clear, relevant, and keyword-aligned.
📂
Business category — Choose the most accurate primary and secondary categories available.
📝
Description — Write for humans first, include your key services and location naturally.
📷
Real photos over generic ones — Upload actual images of your storefront, team, and services in action. Real photos build trust and dramatically increase click rates.
Factor 03
3

How to Respond to Google Reviews

Responding to Google Reviews is vital for building trust, increasing engagement, and enhancing your local reputation. Both positive and negative reviews need a thoughtful, timely response.

😊 For Positive Reviews
1
Thank the reviewer by name
Always start by acknowledging them personally.
"Thank you, Rajesh, for your wonderful feedback!"
2
Reference something specific
Mention a detail from their review to show you read it.
"We're glad you appreciated our quick service."
3
Invite them back
Encourage their return and softly introduce another service.
"We look forward to serving you again soon!"
😟 For Negative Reviews
1
Apologise for their experience
Acknowledge the issue first, without excuses.
"We're sorry to hear about your experience."
2
Stay calm and empathetic
Keep your tone respectful regardless of the complaint.
3
Take the conversation offline
Share direct contact details and involve the owner or manager.
"Please contact our manager directly so we can resolve this."
Why this approach works
Personalised responses prove the reply isn't automated, building genuine trust
Naturally adds location and service keywords to your GMB profile
Softly promotes other services you offer without sounding salesy
Gives unhappy customers a clear resolution path, preventing public arguments
Future customers see that you genuinely care — this converts browsers into buyers
Common Mistakes

What Not To Do

Avoid these mistakes when managing your GMB reviews — each one quietly damages your local reputation over time.

❌ Review response mistakes to avoid
Copy and paste the same response to every review — every reviewer deserves something unique
Sound robotic with generic lines like "Thanks for the review" — it makes your business feel impersonal
Argue or get defensive in public — never fight with customers online
Ignore negative reviews — not responding is worse than a bad response
Respond weeks after the review was posted — timely engagement is everything
Wrapping Up

Conclusion

Optimising your Google My Business listing is one of the highest-leverage actions a local business can take. Done right, it builds long-term trust, improves discoverability, and turns online visibility into real customers walking through your door.

The three areas to focus on 🎯

1
Strengthen Proximity signals — accurate pin, correct service area, suite details, and local markers.
2
Improve CTR through listing optimisation — compelling title, right category, real photos, and a strong description.
3
Respond to reviews professionally — personalised, timely, and empathetic for both positive and negative feedback.
