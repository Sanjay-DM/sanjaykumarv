The Challenge

A real estate client with no social presence — and no audience to advertise to

When this real estate client first came to us, they had one core problem: they had no social media presence whatsoever. No Facebook page. No Instagram. No followers. No content history. Nothing.

This created a fundamental issue for Meta Ads. Meta's algorithm relies on audience signals — past engagement, page followers, pixel data, and content interaction — to efficiently deliver ads to the right people. Without any of this, running paid campaigns would mean paying top dollar for cold, untargeted delivery. The algorithm would have no idea who to show the ads to.

⚠ Starting Conditions
No Facebook or Instagram pages existed — had to be created from scratch
Zero followers, zero engagement history, zero pixel data
Meta's algorithm had no audience signals to work with
Running ads immediately would mean high CPM and poor targeting efficiency
Real estate is a high-intent category — reaching the wrong audience wastes budget fast

The brief was clear: generate WhatsApp conversations from serious property enquiries. But before we could run a single rupee of ads, we had to build the foundation.

The Strategy

A 3-phase funnel: Build → Warm → Convert

Rather than jumping straight into lead generation ads — which would have been expensive and inefficient — we designed a structured 3-phase funnel. Each phase served a specific purpose and fed directly into the next.

Phase 01 — Foundation

Build the pages. Post consistently. Train the algorithm.

Before a single ad was launched, we spent weeks establishing the client's social media presence. This wasn't optional — it was critical infrastructure.

Pages Created
Facebook & Instagram Setup

We created and fully optimised both the Facebook Page and Instagram account — profile photos, cover images, bio, contact details, and call-to-action buttons all configured for a real estate business.

Consistent Posting
Content Calendar Execution

We ran a consistent posting schedule with property showcases, project highlights, location benefits, testimonial posts, and educational content about home buying. The goal was not viral reach — it was to give Meta's algorithm something to learn from. Every post was a data point. Who liked it. Who saved it. Who clicked through. Meta stores all of this.

Property showcase posts with pricing and location highlights
Home-buying tips and local real estate market content
Consistent posting cadence — not sporadic, never dropped
Engagement prompts to build organic interaction signals
Why This Mattered
Audience Signals for Meta's Algorithm

Meta Ads works on audience signals. When you run ads to a page with zero history, the algorithm spends days (and rupees) in a "learning phase" — testing broadly just to figure out who might be interested. By building organic engagement first, we gave Meta a head start. It already knew who engaged with real estate content on our page. When we turned on ads, the learning phase was dramatically shorter and more efficient.

Phase 02 — Awareness

TOF Awareness Campaign — Reaching 231,430 people at ₹2.81 CPM

With the social pages active and content history established, we launched the Top-of-Funnel (TOF) Awareness Campaign — the first paid campaign in the strategy.

The objective was not leads yet. The objective was brand reach and pixel warming. We needed to get the brand in front of the right audience — people in the target geography who matched the profile of a real estate buyer — and let Meta's pixel track who clicked, who visited the website, and who spent time on key pages.

Phase 03 — Conversion

Traffic & Engagement Campaigns — Driving WhatsApp Conversations

With awareness built and the pixel populated, we launched the conversion phase: TOF Traffic and Engagement campaigns optimised for WhatsApp messaging conversations. Rather than a generic "Contact Us" form, WhatsApp was the chosen conversion point — because in the Indian real estate market, buyers prefer conversational, low-friction enquiries over formal lead forms.

TOF TRAFF · 3BHK
Traffic Campaign — 3BHK Properties

Targeted potential buyers specifically interested in 3BHK configurations. Achieved 83 WhatsApp conversations at an average cost of ₹80.04 per conversation — strong performance for a high-ticket real estate category.

Key Takeaways

What this project taught me

This wasn't just a paid ads project. It was a full funnel build — from zero to a converting lead generation machine. The structured approach made all the difference.

01
Never skip the foundation
Running ads to a page with no history is like opening a shop with no signage. Building the social presence first gave the algorithm the signals it needed to perform.
02
Organic content is audience research
Every organic post told Meta who our real audience was. That data became the targeting engine for every paid campaign that followed.
03
Awareness before conversion, always
Cold audiences don't convert. The ₹649 awareness spend that reached 231K people was what made the ₹11K conversion spend so efficient.
04
WhatsApp beats web forms in India
For real estate in India, WhatsApp is the highest-converting touchpoint. People message before they visit. The strategy was built around this buyer behaviour.
05
Segment by product, not just audience
Running 2BHK and 3BHK as separate campaigns ensured creative and messaging matched buyer intent precisely — and prevented audience dilution.
06
The full funnel compounds over time
Each phase built on the last. The awareness campaign fed the conversion campaign. The organic posts fed the awareness campaign. Structure creates compounding returns.
